Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to support@leotio.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and app version (Settings › Apps › FoldInk).

FoldInk

Where are my pictures stored?

Only on your iPhone, in the FoldInk gallery. There is no account and no cloud copy. If you delete the app, your pictures are deleted too, so share or export the ones you want to keep first.

How does folding work?

On iPhone Duo the device itself is the paper: open it to paint, close it to make the print, open it again to see the result. On other iPhones, use the fold slider next to the sheet. The slider is also available on iPhone Duo in the menu, for example as an accessibility alternative.

Does FoldInk cost anything?

No. FoldInk 1.0 is free, without ads and without in-app purchases.

Purchases and refunds

Should we offer purchases in the future, they are handled by Apple. You can request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.
