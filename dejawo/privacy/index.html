Déjà-where: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

In short

Déjà-where turns photos from your own photo library into daily puzzles. The app has no account, no server of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your photos, results and settings are processed and stored only on your device. The app itself sends no data to us.

The exceptions are Apple services the app uses for maps, place names, iCloud Photos and, if you play a live match, SharePlay, as well as the data Apple provides to us as the developer (TestFlight, App Store). They are described below.

What the app processes on your device

  • Photos and their metadata (capture date, location, hidden and screenshot flags) from your photo library, to select suitable photos and build the puzzles. They are read on the device only. The app never uploads your photos, never copies them to a server and never changes them.
  • Image analysis results (for example motif category, check for sensitive content), to choose good puzzle photos and exclude unsuitable ones. Computed on the device with Apple's Vision and SensitiveContentAnalysis frameworks.
  • Game data: puzzles, guesses, points, streaks, statistics, sticker book, blocked photos and the short notes you write about a photo. Stored in the local app database on the device (SwiftData, iCloud sync switched off).
  • Settings (for example reminder, battle duration), to remember your preferences. Stored in the local app settings on the device.
  • Widget snapshot (cropped image of today's photo, solved state, points, streak, place name, year), to show the "Photo of the day" widget. Stored in a container shared by app and widget on the device; deleted when photo access is revoked.

None of this data is sent to us or to third parties. If you use device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer), Apple may include app data in that backup according to your own settings.

Apple services the app uses

The app contains no network code of its own. Some Apple frameworks it uses contact Apple's servers on their own:

  • Apple Maps (MapKit). The map is loaded from Apple. When you type or dictate a place in the search field, the search text is sent to Apple to get suggestions. To show place names (place, region, country), the coordinates of the photo and of your guess are sent to Apple for reverse geocoding. According to Apple, this data is not linked to your Apple Account. See Apple Maps & Privacy.
  • iCloud Photos. If you use iCloud Photos with "Optimize iPhone Storage", iOS may download the full-resolution version of a photo from your iCloud library when the app shows it, just as in the Photos app. This transfer happens between your device and your own iCloud account and is handled by Apple. See the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Notifications. Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device. No push service is used.
  • Sharing. Only if you tap "Send postcard" does the app create a postcard image with the photo you chose, its capture date and a short greeting; the place name is optional and can be switched off in the preview. The image is newly rendered and contains no GPS or EXIF data and no points. It is handed to the iOS share sheet; what happens next depends on the app you choose. Battle results are shared as plain text without any photo.

Live match with friends (SharePlay)

This section applies as soon as the live match is available in your version of the app, and only if you start or join a match in a FaceTime or Messages SharePlay session.

  • The host picks the photos for the match with Apple's photo picker. Only these photos are used.
  • For each round, the host's device sends the other participants a newly encoded copy of the photo (at most 1024 pixels, without GPS or EXIF data) and the order in which it is revealed. After the round it sends the solution: location rounded to about 11 metres (no location for photos taken at home), year, season and, if available, the place name.
  • Nicknames, guesses and points are exchanged between participants during the match. No asset IDs, exact capture dates or other photos from your library are sent.
  • On the participants' devices, the photos are kept in memory only and deleted when the match ends.
  • The transfer is handled by Apple's SharePlay service. We do not receive any of this data. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the match you started or joined). Please only share photos in a match that you are happy for the other participants to see.

Data Apple provides to us

  • TestFlight (beta tests). If you test a beta version via Apple's TestFlight, Apple provides us with the data needed to run the test: the name and email address used for your invitation (not for public links), device model, operating system version, install and session dates, crash reports, and feedback such as screenshots and comments that you choose to send. We use this data only to run the test and fix bugs (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is a stable app) and delete it when it is no longer needed. Apple explains its own processing in the TestFlight app and in the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • App Store. If you agreed in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple provides us with aggregated usage and crash statistics that do not identify you.

Legal basis

Using Apple Maps for search and place names is necessary to provide the game you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Processing on your device happens under your sole control; we do not receive this data.

Contacting us by email

If you write to us, we process your details to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). Emails are stored with our provider STRATO GmbH, Otto-Ostrowski-Straße 7, 10249 Berlin, Germany, under a data processing agreement. We delete the data when it is no longer needed and no statutory retention obligations apply.

Permissions

  • Photo library: required for the puzzles. You can choose full or limited access and change it at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.
  • Notifications: optional, only for the daily reminder.

The app does not request access to your current location, contacts, camera, microphone or tracking (App Tracking Transparency).

What the app does not do

  • no user account or sign-in
  • no analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs from third parties
  • no tracking across apps or websites
  • no server of its own; no transfer of photos, results or metadata to us
  • no sale or sharing of personal data

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all app data from your device (game data, notes, settings, widget snapshot). Revoking photo access removes the widget image. Your photos in the photo library are never changed by the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21), and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Apart from TestFlight data and emails, we store no personal data about you; data on your device is under your sole control. For questions please contact the address above.

Competent supervisory authority: State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information North Rhine-Westphalia (LDI NRW), www.ldi.nrw.de.

Changes

If the app's data processing changes (for example a future family mode via iCloud), we will update this policy before that version is released.
