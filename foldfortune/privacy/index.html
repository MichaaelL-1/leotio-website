FoldFortune Privacy

FoldFortune does not collect any personal data. Your collection stays on your device.

Controller

Michael Leicht, Fährstraße 217, 40221 Düsseldorf, Germany. Email: info@leotio.com. Leotio GmbH is currently being founded and will take over this role once it has been registered.

What the app does and does not do

FoldFortune has no account, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics SDK. It does not upload anything to a FoldFortune server and uses no external AI service.

  • On your device: drawings, fortunes, editing history and settings are stored in the app’s local storage. Device backups you enable (iCloud or computer) may include this data; that is governed by your Apple settings.
  • Good fortune: the object you name and your picture are processed on the device. If Apple Intelligence is available, FoldFortune uses Apple’s on-device model; otherwise it uses prepared texts. Your inputs and fortunes may remain in your local collection.
  • Motion sensors: the device tilt lets wet paint flow. These readings are processed only live and are neither stored nor transmitted.
  • Photos: when you choose to save a picture or video to Photos, FoldFortune asks for add-only access. It does not read your photo library.
  • Sharing: pictures, fortune cards, videos or files leave the app only when you share or save them yourself, to the app you choose. Copies you shared remain even if you delete the original in FoldFortune.
  • Deletion: remove works via the collection and trash. Emptying the trash deletes them from the app; deleting the app removes its local storage.
  • Apple: Apple processes download data under its own responsibility. Depending on your system settings, Apple may share diagnostic data with app developers.

No access to your microphone, contacts or location is needed for these features.
