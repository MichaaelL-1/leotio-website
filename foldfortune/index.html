FoldFortune

Paint. Fold. Be surprised.

A few colours, a folded sheet, and suddenly you see something new. Paint with oil, watercolour or ink, fold the sheet and open it again: the paint is pressed onto the other half. Sometimes a butterfly appears. Sometimes something only you can see.

On iPhone you fold with a slider; on a supported foldable iPhone the device itself becomes the paper. Afterwards, discover your good fortune: a small, friendly thought based on what you see in your picture.

  • Share your picture, a fortune card or a short film with fold animation and optional music
  • No sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases; your collection stays on your device
  • English and German
