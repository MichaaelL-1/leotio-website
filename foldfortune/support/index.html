FoldFortune Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to support@leotio.com. Please describe what happened and include your iPhone model, iOS version and FoldFortune version. Never send passwords.

How does folding work?

Paint on the sheet, move the fold slider all the way closed and open it again. On a supported foldable iPhone you can fold the device itself. Paint is only transferred when the sheet is fully closed.

Where are my pictures?

Open the collection and choose Pictures or Omens. Painted sheets are saved automatically on your device. Keep an omen with “Save”. Deleted works can be restored from the trash until you remove them permanently.

How do I back up my collection?

Use “Save editable file” to keep important pictures in Files as well; you can continue editing them in FoldFortune later. Picture and video exports are finished views. FoldFortune has no automatic cloud sync, so back up important works before you delete the app or change devices.

The video has no sound

Turn on music in the video preview and tap Play. Check the media volume and audio output, including connected headphones, and try the saved video in Photos. If it is still silent, let us know whether the video in Photos has sound.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. Without an available model, omens use prepared texts. Supported devices can write omens directly on the device. Omens are a playful fantasy, not advice.

Does FoldFortune cost anything?

FoldFortune 1.0 contains no ads and no in-app purchases.
