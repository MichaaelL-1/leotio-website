Déjà-when Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to support@leotio.com. Please describe what happened and include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version and Déjà-when version. Never send passwords or photos you do not want to share.

Why does it say there are not enough photos?

Déjà-when needs photos with a place and a date that are at least a year old. Screenshots, receipts, documents and blurry shots are left out. If you gave limited access, share a few more photos in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Déjà-when.

How are points calculated?

Up to 10,000 points per puzzle for place and time together. The closer your pin and the closer the year, the more points; fewer glimpses earn more. The rules page in the app explains every step.

Can I stop a photo from appearing?

Yes. In the result, choose “Never show this photo again”. It stays in your library; Déjà-when just won’t use it for puzzles anymore.

What is shared when I send a postcard?

Only the postcard image you see in the preview and a short greeting. The image is newly drawn, without GPS or EXIF data and without points; you can switch off the place name.

The daily reminder does not arrive

Check that notifications for Déjà-when are allowed in Settings → Notifications and that the reminder is switched on in the app. Focus modes can hold reminders back.

Does Déjà-when cost anything?

Déjà-when 1.0 is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
