Déjà-when

Your photos. When and where?

Coming soon to the App Store

Every day, Déjà-when picks one photo you took yourself and turns it into a small puzzle. First you get just a glimpse. Guess the year and the season, then drop a pin on the map. The closer you are, the more points you get, and suddenly you remember the summer, the trip, the people.

For places you have photographed again and again, the game only asks “when?”. Play the Battle against the clock, keep your streak going, or send a memory postcard to someone who was there.

  • Plays only with your own photos; they are analysed on your device and never uploaded
  • No sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking
  • iPhone and iPad, English and German
