Déjà-when Privacy

Déjà-when does not collect any personal data. Your photos, results and settings stay on your device.

What the app does and does not do

Déjà-when has no account, no server of its own, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics SDK. It never uploads your photos and contains no network code of its own.

  • Photos: the app reads photos and their metadata (capture date, location) from your library to build puzzles. Image analysis (motif, unsuitable content) runs on the device with Apple’s Vision and SensitiveContentAnalysis frameworks. You can give full or limited access and change it at any time in Settings.
  • On your device: puzzles, guesses, points, streaks, statistics, blocked photos and settings are stored in the app’s local database (iCloud sync off). Device backups you enable (iCloud or computer) may include this data; that is governed by your Apple settings.
  • Widget: a cropped image of today’s photo, solved state, points, streak, place name and year are stored in a container shared by app and widget on the device and deleted when photo access is revoked.
  • Apple Maps: the map is loaded from Apple. Search text you type and the coordinates of the photo and of your guess (for place names) are sent to Apple. Apple says it does not link this data to your Apple Account.
  • iCloud Photos: with “Optimize iPhone Storage”, iOS may download the full-resolution photo from your own iCloud library when the app shows it, just like in the Photos app.
  • Notifications: the daily reminder is a local notification; no push service is used.
  • Sharing: only when you tap “Send postcard” does the app create a newly drawn postcard image with the photo you chose and a short greeting, without GPS or EXIF data and without points. Battle results are shared as plain text without a photo. What happens next depends on the app you choose.
  • Dia-Runde (SharePlay): photos for a shared round are newly encoded without EXIF or GPS data and sent directly between the participants’ devices via Apple SharePlay. After each round all participants see the solution (place and year).
  • Deletion: deleting the app removes all its data from your device. Your photos in the library are never changed.

No access to your current location, contacts, camera or microphone is needed, and no tracking permission is requested.
