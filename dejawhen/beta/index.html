Déjà-when Beta

Play with your own photos for two weeks, before anyone else.

Déjà-when turns one photo you took yourself into a small daily puzzle: when was it, and where? First you get a glimpse, then you guess the year and the season and drop a pin on the map. The closer you are, the more points you get. There is a Battle against the clock, a sticker book, a postcard to send, and a shared round over FaceTime.

What testers do

Play for two weeks, starting November 3. In week one the daily puzzle, the widget and the reminder; in week two the Battle, the sticker book, sharing a postcard, and a shared round with someone in the same room. At the end of each week I send ten short questions. Two sentences per answer are plenty. If something looks odd, take a screenshot in TestFlight and send it straight from there.

Your photos

Your photos are analysed on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework. Nothing is uploaded, and Déjà-when has no servers. Only when you tap “Send postcard” does a photo leave the device, as a picture with your greeting and without location data.

Join

Join the beta in TestFlight

You need Apple's free TestFlight app, an iPhone or iPad with iOS 27, and a photo library with photos that have a date and a place. Places are limited to 25 testers. After the release this page will point to the App Store.
